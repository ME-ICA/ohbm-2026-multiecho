Introduction

Accumulation of misfolded amyloid-beta (Aβ) protein is a main characteristic in the development of Alzheimer's disease and several treatments have been investigated in order to reduce it's production or removal from the brain. Our group previously developed a method to increase brain solute efflux using vasodilation with NO-donor in mice.1 Furthermore, mechanical stimulation by whole brody vibration and piezo-1 reseptor stimulation2 also significantly improve memory and reduce AD cognitive decline. In this study we evaluated whether a combined  vasomechanic treatment could be used to increase human brain solute efflux in order to reduce Aβ burden from the brain.

Methods

After Tukija/Fimean national research approval and informed consent, we studied in healthy adults (n = 29, (mean age: 44 ± 10 years; 15 fem) using sublingual NO and Piezo1 receptor targeted mechanotransductive whole body vibrations (WBVp). We assessed their effects on brain fluid dynamics and CNS to blood protein efflux using multimodal neuroimaging and blood biomarker analysis. We scanned the subjects with 3T dark fluid 3D spc (DF) MRI for relative brain solute changes used for glymphatic parasagital dural space chages. Ultrafast MREG (TR = 100 ms) whole brain scanning was used to quantify changes in brain pulsations driving I/CSF.  We took advantage of the fact that dark-fluid MRI can detect protein-rich fluid content, as demonstrated in prior studies using FLAIR/dark-CSF sequences to assess CSF protein load (Eide & Ringstad 2020;Albayram et al., 2022), to characterize physiological changes in brain water and macromolecule distribution. These measurements were combined with quantification of brain-derived proteins entering the bloodstream that are relevant to neurodegeneration, including Aβ40, Aβ42, and glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) using Quanterix’s Single Molecule Array (Simoa) HD-X Analyzer targeted for neurogenerative plasma protein detection.

 The brain structural DF MRI data was analysed for group level intensity comparisons baseline vs. after NO+WBVp treatment The blood samples were analyzed and correlated to blood pressure drop levels. The NO effect on brain pulsation powers of banpassed power of vasomotor < 0.1 Hz, respiratory 0.3 Hz and cardiovascular 1 Hz were analyzed statistically before vs. after NO medication. Statistical analyses were performed using FSL Randomise TFCE corrected FWER p< 0.05)

Results

The results indicate that solutes can be removed from the whole human brain, Fig 1. f. The brain image gets darker in DF (g) and CSF volume increases (h). Also the brain DF MMRI intesity changes alter as a function of age (i-j).  

Figure 2 below shows FFT power spectrograms and whole brain FFT spectra, where the red post treatment curve indicates a selective increase in low frequency (LF) 0.1 Hz Traube-Hering vasomotor waves and respiration pulse power but reduced cardiac pulsation power in the brain during NO vasodilation. 

Conclusion

Our results show that human brain solute efflux of soluble Aβ40 and Aβ42 can be increased with combined vasomechanic NO+WBVp treatments that increase brain vasomotor waves and respiratory pulsations.
